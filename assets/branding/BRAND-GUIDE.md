# ModGlow brand guide

## Moderator authored image comment
The generated image uses a true AMOLED black canvas with a smoked charcoal surface, a fine warm yellow illuminated edge, and a restrained yellow haze. The first row contains the ModGlow app icon and name, moderator badge, timestamp, and pinned marker. The comment body is larger than typical Reddit comment text and uses a high contrast warm white. Inline Markdown emphasis is preserved as bold, italic, or bold italic.

The design uses restrained SVG shadows for text legibility and a soft ambient yellow glow. The surface remains nearly black. It avoids a broad grey fog, heavy gradients, and excessive neon bloom. The card grows vertically with wrapped text.

## Palette
- AMOLED canvas: `#000000`
- Smoked surface: `#080808` to `#101010`
- Main text: `#F2F0E8`
- Warm metadata: `#C9C5B5`
- Neon yellow: `#FFE45C`
- Pale highlight: `#FFEFA3`
- Muted gold: `#A99B62`
- Badge surface: `#29230D`

## Typography
IBM Plex Sans is bundled for Latin glyphs. Noto Sans Malayalam and Noto Sans Tamil remain bundled for those scripts. The comment body uses 64px on a 1536px canvas with 82px line height. Bold, italic, and bold italic Markdown runs are retained.

## Other listing assets
The app icon, banner, wordmarks, and promotional panels remain unchanged. Promotional panels are marketing artwork, not screenshots of the live app.
