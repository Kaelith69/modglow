## 1. What ModGlow does

ModGlow lets eligible subreddit moderators create, edit, and delete announcement comments. It creates an announcement image, can add formatted text below the image, and publishes the result as a comment from the ModGlow app account.

ModGlow is a tool for moderators. It does not make moderation decisions for you and does not publish announcements automatically.

## 2. Who may use it

You may use ModGlow only where you have the Reddit permissions required by the app and the subreddit. The app checks moderator permissions before allowing announcement actions.

You are responsible for making sure that you are authorised to publish, edit, or delete the content and that your use follows your community's rules.

## 3. Your content and actions

You are responsible for the text, links, and other content you enter.

Before publishing, check that:
- You have permission to make the announcement.
- The announcement is accurate and appropriate for the community.
- Any links are correct and safe.
- The content follows Reddit's User Agreement, Reddit Rules, Moderator Code of Conduct, and applicable policies.

When you select **Publish**, ModGlow submits the announcement as a comment from the ModGlow app account. The comment is distinguished as a moderator comment and pinned where Reddit permits.

Your announcement becomes Reddit content and is subject to Reddit's terms, policies, moderation tools, and retention practices. Do not submit content that you do not have the right to publish.

## 4. Editing and deletion

ModGlow can edit or delete only comments that it can verify as its own managed announcements.

Editing changes the existing comment. Deletion is permanent and requires a separate confirmation. You are responsible for checking the correct comment before confirming deletion.

Announcements without a valid saved ModGlow management record may not be editable or deletable through the app.
